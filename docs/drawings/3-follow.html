3. Follow

The hook, the loop and the turns while Vibe follows.

In words

  1. Vibe finishes a turn.
  2. Before the turn closes, Vibe runs the hook. The hook does not return: it waits on the meeting.
  3. Someone speaks; the hook returns the line and says "do not stop", so Vibe works on in the same turn and answers on its page.
  4. This can happen 3 times. After the third, Vibe closes the turn and nobody waits.
  5. The loop notices Vibe is idle and, within 30 seconds, starts an empty turn. When that turn ends, we are back at step 2.

The loop fires only while Vibe is idle; while the hook waits the turn is not over, so the loop stays quiet (read from Vibe’s source, not tested).

What is tested. Only the hook has run in Vibe. The skill, the loop next to the hook, and leaving are written but not tried.
