In words

  1. Peter tells Vibe: "join the meeting".
  2. Vibe reads the skill.
  3. The skill writes the agent-link file, and starts the loop.
  4. The hook needs the agent-link file. It waits for lines on the meeting feed and hands them over to Vibe.
  5. The loop starts a new turn in Vibe.
  6. Vibe answers on the agent’s page.
  7. People in the meeting talk on the feed and read the page.
